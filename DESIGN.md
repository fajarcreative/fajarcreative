## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-energy agency identity engineered for high conversion, trust, and digital craftsmanship. Built to serve fast-growing enterprises, local businesses, and modern startups, the visual language merges enterprise stability with energetic execution. 

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The system combines **Corporate Modern** with **Vibrant SaaS Precision**:
- **Purity & Contrast:** Crisp, ultra-clean surfaces accented by deep slate tones ensure effortless readability and immediate trust.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Punchy accents (sunset warmth and conversion emerald) punctuate the grounded azure foundation to guide user eyes straight toward conversion funnels.
- **Tactile Softness:** Balanced geometry with generous radii (`rounded-xl` and fully rounded pills) removes stiffness, producing an inviting, optimistic, and forward-looking digital presence.

## Colors

The palette balances technical dependability with persuasive marketing accents:

- **Primary (`#0284c7` - Royal Azure):** Establishes the digital core. Used for brand headers, primary callouts, hero focal points, active states, and focal iconography.
- **Secondary (`#ea580c` - Sunset Amber / Tangerine):** High-urgency accent. Applied to promotional pill banners, primary high-priority CTAs ("Mulai Proyek", "Konsultasi Gratis"), and attention-grabbing metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#16a34a` - Conversion Emerald):** Trust and conversion driver. Reserved for WhatsApp triggers, success feedback, verified credentials, and high-impact ROI highlights.
- **Neutral (`#0f172a` - Deep Slate):** Anchors high-contrast typographic hierarchy against pure white (`#ffffff`) and cool slate tint backgrounds (`#f8fafc` to `#f1f5f9`).

### Palette Rules
- Surface layers must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 7:1 for body copy against light backgrounds.
- Never use the tertiary green for decorative flourishes; preserve its semantic authority for growth, live indicators, and WhatsApp actions.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a clean, contemporary rhythm with wide apertures and humanist geometry that renders crisply across mobile devices and high-density screens.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (700 and 800) with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) establish authority and modern technological confidence.
- **Body:** Neutral weights (400) set at 1.5 to 1.55 line-height multiples ensure effortless scanability for service packages, case studies, and long-form proposal details.
- **Labels & Badges:** Semi-bold to bold weights with uppercase styling and positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.05em`) provide distinction for category tags, pricing chips, and client badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to keep agency portfolio showcases, service stacks, and pricing tiers organized and breathable.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, max container width `1200px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`.

### Spacing Application
- Component internal padding strictly uses `space-sm` (8px) through `space-lg` (24px).
- Inter-section vertical rhythm relies on stacked tiers: `space-xl` (40px) on mobile scaling to `4rem`–`6rem` on desktop to give portfolio visuals and agency value propositions ample negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp, layered clarity through subtle multi-layer ambient tints and structural borders.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (Layer 0):** Pure `#ffffff` or cool `#f8fafc`.
2. **Card Surfaces (Layer 1):** Solid `#ffffff` paired with a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`) and an ultra-diffused ambient shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Elevated CTAs (Layer 2):** Applied to floating WhatsApp triggers, sticky navigation, and modal dialogues:
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
   - Subtle blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` on 85% opacity surface containers.

### Hover Transitions
Interactive cards translate up by `2px` to `4px` while the shadow shifts to a soft tinted glow using the primary azure or sunset amber tone.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified curvature token set to maintain a clean, approachable, and high-tech feel:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard form inputs, dropdowns, and micro elements.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1rem / 16px to 1.5rem / 24px):** Applied to service cards, portfolio previews, and pricing containers.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Applied exclusively to category badges, conversion buttons, status indicators, and avatar wrappers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Conversion):** Pill or `rounded-xl` shape, solid energetic sunset orange (`#ea580c`) or royal azure (`#0284c7`), bold text (`label-lg`), with a subtle inner highlight and active press compression (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, 1.5px border matching primary azure or deep slate, text in high-contrast neutral.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Solid conversion emerald (`#16a34a`) background with pure white typography, integrated brand icon, and persistent floating anchor.

### Chips & Badges
- **Vibrant Status Badges:** Pill-shaped, tinted backgrounds (`#0284c715`, `#ea580c15`, `#16a34a15`) paired with full-saturation text in the respective accent color (`label-md`).
- **Feature Tags:** Subtle slate tint (`#f1f5f9`), slate text (`#334155`), clean 1px border (`#e2e8f0`).

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Enclosed with `rounded-xl` (16px–24px) corners, 1px perimeter border (`#e2e8f0`), padded with `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (40px).
- Incorporates high-contrast headline titles, muted body descriptions, and an anchored accent link or badge at the bottom.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height of 48px, rounded 8px–12px, border in neutral tint (`#cbd5e1`), with a 2px royal azure glow on `:focus-visible`.
- Labels float top-left in `label-sm` or `label-md` with Slate-700 (`#334155`) for accessibility.

### Portfolio Showcase Modules
- Aspect ratios locked to 16:10 or 4:3 with `rounded-xl` clipping masks, overlaying semi-transparent action pills on hover.
